# AI Audit Systems

## Overview

The AI Audit module explores how artificial intelligence systems may assist in analyzing governance transparency, public accountability, and structured civic infrastructure.

This module is intended to support:
- governance classification
- transparency scoring
- treasury analysis
- procedural consistency checks
- civic metadata indexing

---

# Potential Applications

## Governance Analysis
AI systems may classify:
- zoning actions
- treasury actions
- procurement activity
- infrastructure decisions
- governance procedures

## Transparency Scoring
AI systems may evaluate:
- public accessibility
- documentation completeness
- agenda consistency
- disclosure quality

## Civic Infrastructure Mapping
AI systems may help structure:
- municipal metadata
- governance schemas
- interoperability standards
- public infrastructure records

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective is to explore AI-auditable governance systems capable of supporting:
- machine-readable governance
- civic transparency
- interoperable public systems
- programmable civic infrastructure
- privacy-preserving accountability
