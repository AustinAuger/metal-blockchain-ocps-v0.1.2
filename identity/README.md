# Identity Systems

## Overview

The Identity module explores privacy-preserving identity systems for governance, civic participation, and interoperable public infrastructure.

The objective is to support:
- verifiable civic credentials
- privacy-preserving compliance
- decentralized identity standards
- machine-readable governance participation
- selective disclosure systems

---

# Core Concepts

## Verifiable Credentials
Users may prove attributes without exposing unnecessary personal information.

Examples:
- residency status
- voting eligibility
- licensing credentials
- municipal participation rights

## Privacy Preservation
Identity systems should minimize unnecessary surveillance while supporting legal and procedural accountability.

## Governance Participation
Identity systems may support:
- governance voting
- civic participation
- public authentication
- procedural verification

# Long-Term Vision

This module explores how identity infrastructure may evolve toward:
- decentralized identity systems
- zero-knowledge verification
- interoperable governance participation
- privacy-preserving civic infrastructure
