# 📊 DAO Nutrition Label Scoring Methodology

Version: v0.4 Draft

This document defines how DAO Nutrition Label scores are assigned.

---

# Scoring Scale

All categories are scored from:

| Score | Interpretation |
|---------|---------|
| 0-2 | Minimal disclosure |
| 3-4 | Limited disclosure |
| 5-6 | Moderate disclosure |
| 7-8 | Strong disclosure |
| 9-10 | Comprehensive disclosure |

---

# Governance Transparency

Measures visibility into decision-making processes.

Factors:

- Public governance documentation
- Voting mechanisms
- Proposal history
- Governance participation metrics
- Upgrade authority disclosure

---

# Technical Transparency

Measures visibility into system architecture.

Factors:

- Open-source code
- Technical documentation
- Whitepapers
- Architecture diagrams
- Public repositories

---

# Treasury Transparency

Measures visibility into organizational assets.

Factors:

- Treasury reporting
- Reserve disclosures
- Spending reports
- Asset allocation visibility
- Treasury governance rules

---

# Tokenomics Disclosure

Measures visibility into economic design.

Factors:

- Supply schedule
- Emission model
- Distribution model
- Vesting schedules
- Utility explanations

---

# Compliance Readiness

Measures readiness for regulated environments.

Factors:

- AML policies
- KYC integration
- Regulatory disclosures
- Risk management processes
- Compliance documentation

---

# Custody & Asset Protection

Measures protection of user and organizational assets.

Factors:

- Custody disclosures
- Key management policies
- Security controls
- Asset protection procedures

---

# Operational Resilience

Measures ability to operate reliably.

Factors:

- Infrastructure maturity
- Validator/network stability
- Disaster recovery planning
- Business continuity practices

---

# Composite Score

Overall scores are calculated as a weighted combination of all categories.

Future versions may support automated scoring and on-chain attestations.

---

# Purpose

DAO Nutrition Label is designed to provide:

- Transparency benchmarking
- Governance visibility
- Regulatory readiness assessments
- Cross-chain comparisons
- Institutional due diligence support
