# 🌐 MiCA Multi-Chain Readiness Comparison

This document demonstrates how the DAO Nutrition Label v0.3 (MiCA Module) can be applied across multiple blockchain ecosystems to evaluate regulatory transparency and readiness.

| Ecosystem | Score | Classification |

---

## 🧠 Interpretation

### 🥇 Highest Readiness: WebAuth Wallet (82/100)
Strong identity architecture and compliance alignment make this the most MiCA-ready component in the ecosystem.

### 🥈 Metal Blockchain (76/100)
Strong institutional and compliance foundation, but requires improved treasury and governance transparency.

### 🥉 XPR Network (71/100)
Solid technical and compliance alignment with strong identity layer advantages.

---

## ⚖️ Key Insight

Across all evaluated systems, the primary gap is not technical capability.

It is:

> **standardized transparency reporting (especially treasury + governance + risk disclosure).**

---

## 🧭 What MiCA changes

MiCA effectively standardizes:

- Asset issuance disclosure
- Reserve transparency (for stablecoins)
- Operational governance expectations
- Licensing requirements for service providers

This means ecosystems with structured transparency frameworks (like DAO Nutrition Label) are better positioned for compliance alignment.
