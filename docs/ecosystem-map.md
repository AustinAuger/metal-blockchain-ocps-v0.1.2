# DAO Nutrition Label Ecosystem Map

## Overview

The DAO Nutrition Label ecosystem explores machine-readable governance infrastructure for decentralized organizations, civic systems, treasury transparency, identity frameworks, AI auditability, and interoperable public infrastructure.

---

# Ecosystem Modules

## DAO
Governance transparency systems for DAOs and decentralized organizations.

## Civic
Municipal governance transparency and civic infrastructure systems.

## AI Audit
AI-based governance analysis, transparency scoring, and procedural auditability.

## Identity
Privacy-preserving governance identity and verifiable credential systems.

## Treasury
Transparent treasury infrastructure and programmable financial governance.

## Interoperability
Shared governance schemas, machine-readable protocols, and infrastructure interoperability.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective is to support the evolution of:
- machine-readable governance
- AI-auditable public systems
- interoperable civic infrastructure
- privacy-preserving participation
- programmable governance systems
